Quick Formula & Direct Answer (2026 Standard)

To convert an hourly rate to an annual salary for a full-time job (40 hours per week), multiply your hourly rate by 2,080 working hours:

Annual Salary = Hourly Wage × Hours Worked Per Week × Weeks Worked Per Year

For example, $25/hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000/year gross ($4,333/month or $2,000 biweekly). With 2 weeks of unpaid time off (2,000 hours), total gross pay equals $50,000/year.

How to Convert Hourly Pay to Annual Salary: Detailed Mathematical Guide

Converting an hourly rate to an annual salary is one of the most common financial calculations for job seekers, contractors, and human resources professionals. While the basic calculation appears straightforward, factors such as paid time off, unpaid vacation, overtime eligibility, and statutory payroll tax withholdings have a substantial impact on actual take-home earnings.

The Standard 2,080 Hours Rule (Paid Holidays and Vacation)

In the United States, human resources departments, payroll systems, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) calculate full-time employment based on a 40-hour work week across 52 weeks in a calendar year:

40 hours/week × 52 weeks/year = 2,080 working hours per year

If your employer offers paid time off (PTO)—including paid vacation days, paid sick leave, and federal holidays—your annual gross pay is calculated directly by multiplying your hourly rate by 2,080. For instance, at $30 an hour, your annual compensation equals $62,400 per year.

The 2,000 Hours Benchmark (Unpaid Time Off or Independent Contractors)

Many hourly workers, temporary staffing employees, and 1099 independent contractors do not receive paid holidays or paid vacations. If you take 2 weeks of unpaid leave during the year, your active working schedule is 50 weeks:

40 hours/week × 50 weeks/year = 2,000 working hours per year

Using the 2,000 hours benchmark makes mental math exceptionally easy: simply double your hourly wage and add three zeros. For example, at $25 an hour, doubling equals 50, which translates to $50,000 per year without paid time off.

Paycheck Frequencies: Biweekly vs Semi-Monthly

Understanding your pay frequency is crucial for budgeting mortgage payments, rent, and utility bills:

  • Biweekly (Every 2 Weeks): 26 pay periods per year. Each paycheck covers 80 hours. Most months you receive 2 paychecks, but two months per year contain a "three-paycheck bonus" month.
  • Semi-Monthly (Twice Per Month): 24 pay periods per year, commonly paid on the 1st and 15th, or 15th and 30th. Each paycheck covers approximately 86.67 hours.
  • Weekly: 52 pay periods per year. Ideal for cash flow, covering 40 regular hours per paycheck.
  • Monthly: 12 pay periods per year. Covers 173.33 hours per paycheck.

Understanding 2026 Taxes and Net Take-Home Pay

Your gross salary is not what reaches your bank account. Every US paycheck is subject to mandatory federal and state withholdings:

  1. Federal Income Tax: Progressive brackets ranging from 10% to 37% after subtracting the 2026 standard deduction ($14,600 for single filers).
  2. Social Security Tax (OASDI): 6.2% of gross earnings up to the statutory 2026 wage cap of $184,500.
  3. Medicare Tax: 1.45% on all gross earnings, plus an additional 0.9% surtax on earnings exceeding $200,000 for single filers.
  4. State and Local Income Taxes: Varies significantly by jurisdiction. States such as Texas, Florida, Nevada, Washington, and Tennessee have 0% state income tax on wages, while states like California and New York levy progressive rates up to 13.3% and 10.9%.

Frequently Asked Questions (Hourly to Salary Conversion)

How do you calculate hourly wage to annual salary?

To convert an hourly wage to an annual salary, multiply your hourly rate by the number of hours worked per week, then multiply by 52 weeks. For a standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week, multiply your hourly rate by 2,080 hours (40 × 52). For example, $25 per hour × 2,080 hours = $52,000 per year gross salary.

How many work hours are in a year?

A standard full-time work year consists of 2,080 hours (40 hours per week × 52 weeks). If you take 2 weeks of unpaid vacation or unpaid time off, your working hours total 2,000 hours per year (40 hours × 50 weeks).

How much is $25 an hour per year after taxes?

At $25 an hour ($52,000 gross annual pay), a single filer in 2026 pays approximately $4,057 in Federal Income Tax and $3,978 in FICA payroll taxes. In states with no state income tax (such as Texas, Florida, or Nevada), your estimated take-home pay is approximately $43,965 per year ($3,664/month or $1,691 biweekly).

What is the difference between biweekly and semi-monthly pay?

Biweekly pay occurs every two weeks, resulting in 26 paychecks per year. Semi-monthly pay occurs twice per month (typically on the 1st and 15th, or 15th and last day of the month), resulting in 24 paychecks per year. Semi-monthly paychecks are slightly larger because annual salary is divided by 24 rather than 26.

Is hourly pay or salaried compensation better?

Hourly employees are legally protected under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and entitled to 1.5x overtime pay for any hours worked beyond 40 per week. Salaried exempt employees receive a guaranteed regular paycheck regardless of hours worked, but often do not receive additional pay for working evenings or weekends.
