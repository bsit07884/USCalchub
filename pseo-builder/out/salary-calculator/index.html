How this calculation works

Federal income tax

2026 IRS progressive brackets (10%–37%). Standard deduction $14,600 for single filers. Source: IRS Pub 15-T.

FICA — Social Security + Medicare

Social Security 6.2% on first $168,600. Medicare 1.45% on all income. Additional 0.9% above $200k.

State income tax

9 states charge 0% (TX, FL, NV, WY, WA, TN, SD, AK, NH). California tops at 13.3%, New York at 10.9%.

Private by design

All calculations run in your browser. No data stored, no signup required, no ads — ever.

Frequently asked questions

Which state has the highest take-home pay on a $75,000 salary?
Texas, Florida, Nevada, and Wyoming — all with zero state income tax — give the highest take-home at approximately $60,900/year on a $75,000 salary in 2026.
How much less do I take home in California vs Texas?
On a $75,000 salary, California residents take home approximately $5,475 less per year than Texas residents due to California's 9.3% state income tax rate at that income level.
Are these figures accurate for 2026?
Yes. All federal brackets use 2026 IRS Publication 15-T rates and the $14,600 standard deduction. State rates are sourced from Tax Foundation 2026 data, updated for states that cut rates effective January 1, 2026.
