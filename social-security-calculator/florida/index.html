Is Social Security Taxed in Florida in 2026?

Reviewed by Muzaffar Ali, Financial Content Team · September 2026 ·SSA 2026·Tax Foundation 2026
Direct Answer

Is Social Security taxed in Florida in 2026? No. Social Security retirement benefits are 100% tax-free at the state level in Florida. Florida has no state personal income tax on any retirement income, pensions, or withdrawals, paired with a moderate 0.78% effective property tax rate.

Understanding Florida's 2026 Retirement Tax Landscape

Florida does not tax Social Security benefits at the state level. In fact, Florida has no personal income tax at all under Article VII, Section 5 of the Florida Constitution. Retirement income from all sources—including traditional 401(k) and IRA withdrawals, private pensions, government pensions, and investment earnings—is completely free from state income tax. Homeowners face a moderate effective property tax rate of 0.78%, and permanent residents can shield up to $50,000 of home value through Florida's Homestead Exemption.

Frequently Asked Questions (Florida)

Is Social Security taxed in Florida?

No. Florida does not tax Social Security benefits. Florida has no state personal income tax, so all Social Security payments are 100% exempt from state taxes.

Are pensions and 401(k) withdrawals taxed in Florida?

No. Florida levies zero state income tax on private pensions, public pensions, 401(k) withdrawals, traditional IRA distributions, or capital gains.

How high are property taxes for retirees in Florida?

Florida's statewide effective property tax rate is approximately 0.78%, which is below the national average. Florida also offers a Homestead Exemption shielding up to $50,000 of assessed home value for permanent primary residents.
