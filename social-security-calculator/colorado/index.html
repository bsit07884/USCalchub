Is Social Security Taxed in Colorado in 2026?

Direct Answer

Is Social Security taxed in Colorado in 2026? Yes. Yes, but benefits are 100% exempt for taxpayers aged 65 and older, and partially exempt for those aged 55 to 64 below state AGI limits.

How Colorado's Social Security Tax Rule Works

Fully exempt at age 65+. Ages 55-64 fully exempt if AGI is at or below the threshold; above it, only a partial deduction applies. Under 55, no special SS exemption.
